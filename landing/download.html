What you need.

  • A computer that can stay on — a desktop, an old laptop, or a Raspberry Pi on a shelf all work.
  • Your media files, dropped into the vault folder.
  • Optional: free Cloudflare Tunnel for a public URL, and your own Stripe or PayPal keys if you want to get paid.

After you install.

Three steps to your first broadcast
  1. Run the app — the setup wizard walks you through creating your station.
  2. Drop media into your vault; Paperweight scans and schedules it.
  3. Turn on the tunnel to go public, and share your station URL.
